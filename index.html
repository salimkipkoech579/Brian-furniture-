🌲 Kenyan Hardwoods

Our Wood Selection Guide

All timber used at Brian Furniture is sustainably sourced and kiln-dried to under 12% moisture to prevent cracking or warping in local climates.

African Mahogany

Deep reddish-brown luster with dense grain figure and heirloom durability. Resists scratches and ages with rich patina.

Best for: Master beds, dining suites, executive desks

East African Mvule (Iroko)

High natural oil content offering exceptional resistance to moisture, termites, and fungal decay. Warm golden-brown color.

Best for: Heavy dining tops, kitchen counters, outdoor sets

Kenyan Cypress & Cedar

Aromatic and lightweight yet rigid structural timber. Takes paint and satin stains cleanly without bleeding.

Best for: Bedframe skeletons, shoe racks, wardrobe framing

Wild Acacia

Striking contrasting grain with organic live edges. Rock-solid surface density with natural dramatic character.

Best for: Live-edge centerpieces, coffee tables, bar surfaces
